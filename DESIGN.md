## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, institutional PropTech and financial operating environment built for regional property managers, real estate syndicates, institutional landlords, and tenants. The visual tone bridges Swiss-rationalist structure with Middle Eastern financial solidity. It rejects disposable, overly playful consumer tropes in favor of deep-seated authority, fiscal transparency, and effortless operational clarity.

The visual dialect pairs a clean, content-first architectural grid with subtle glassmorphic surface treatments and dual-plane isometric cues derived directly from the faceted brand mark. Surfaces feel architectural and grounded: expansive white and crisp mint-tinted fields are grounded by deep forest slate accents, razor-sharp metric cards, and tactile data containers. The platform communicates stability, regulatory compliance, and enterprise durability while remaining agile and effortless across mobile and desktop interfaces.

## Colors

The palette operates on high-contrast emerald and charcoal foundations. 

- **Primary (`#16654c`)**: Reserved for core brand anchors, primary action buttons, key metrics, active state borders, and dominant navigation nodes.
- **Secondary (`#248463`)**: Used for interactive highlights, progressive badges, chart vectors, positive financial flows, and secondary calls to action.
- **Tertiary (`#0f766e`)**: Deployed across analytics data visualizations, multi-unit occupancy indicators, and tertiary functional tags.
- **Neutral (`#1f2937`)**: The cornerstone for high-contrast typography, high-priority monetary values, and structural dividers.
- **Named Surfaces**:
  - `surface-mint-subtle` (`#f0fdf4`) delivers background tinting behind hero metric modules and analytical card headers.
  - `surface-mint-card` (`#ecfdf5`) acts as the resting surface for highlighted financial vouchers, paid status tokens, and download banner containers.
  - `brand-charcoal` (`#3e403f`) maintains parity with the master logo mark for secondary body prose, metadata labels, and structural UI rules.

## Typography

The typographic engine relies on **IBM Plex Sans** (with full native support for **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** in RTL contexts). This pairing provides an engineered, authoritative aesthetic that performs seamlessly across high-density financial ledgers and sweeping editorial landing page sections.

- **RTL & Script Harmony**: In RTL mode, base leading is expanded by +2px on body sizes to respect Arabic ascenders and descenders. Numbers within financial vouchers and rent tables use tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve optical alignment across balance sheets.
- **Hierarchical Discipline**: Display hero styles are restricted to conversion landing points and app marketing claims. High-density property dashboards and table headers prioritize `headline-sm` down to `label-md` to preserve vertical screen efficiency.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout architecture employs a structured 12-column fluid grid system across desktop screens, adapting to an 8-column format on tablet and a 4-column stack on mobile devices.

- **Grid Geometry**: Maximum content width is capped at `1280px` to maintain optimal data reading lines. Structural sections span full bleed with inner content bound by standard margins.
- **RTL Mirroring**: In Arabic viewports, the grid axis reverses cleanly along the horizontal plane. Navigation elements, search bars, brand logos, and sequential progress milestones invert without visual distortion or asymmetrical padding offsets.
- **Density Rules**: Data-dense modules (such as unit occupancy matrices and payment reconciliation vouchers) compress internal padding using `space-xs` and `space-sm`, whereas landing page narrative sections leverage `space-lg` and `space-xl` to ensure breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through an interplay of structured planar surfaces, soft forest-tinted ambient shadows, and crisp glassmorphism highlights rather than opaque dark drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Default page backdrop (`#FFFFFF` or subtle mint `#f0fdf4`). Contains zero shadow and no borders.
- **Level 1 (Property & Ledger Cards)**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.08)`, shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(22, 101, 76, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(22, 101, 76, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Dropdowns)**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(22, 101, 76, 0.16)`, shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(22, 101, 76, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(22, 101, 76, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sticky Navigation)**: Frosted glass finish using `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`, shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.1), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced **Rounded (Level 2)** shape standard:
- Standard interactive elements, form fields, and status indicators receive `0.5rem` (8px) border radius.
- Cards, summary panels, and promotional modal wrappers step up to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- App preview shells and hero conversion pods utilize `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- Status badges and pill indicators utilize fully continuous curvature (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to maintain immediate visual contrast against geometric card corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Solid `#16654c` background with crisp white typography, `0.5rem` radius, and 44px minimum touch target height (`px-5 py-2.5`). Hover state transitions to `#248463` with an elevation shift to Level 2.
- **Secondary CTA**: Transparent fill with a `1.5px` border in `#16654c`, `#16654c` text, and subtle `#f0fdf4` tint on hover.
- **App Download Buttons**: Deep charcoal (`#1f2937`) pill or rounded container featuring metallic icon glyphs (App Store / Google Play), dual-line typography (micro-label + store bold), and subtle emerald hover halo.

### Chips & Badges
- **Occupancy & Payment Chips**: Enclosed within 24px height pills (`rounded-full`). 
  - "Occupied" / "Paid": Background `#ecfdf5`, text `#16654c`, dot `#248463`.
  - "Vacant" / "Due": Background `#fef3c7`, text `#b45309`, dot `#d97706`.
  - "Overdue": Background `#fee2e2`, text `#b91c1c`, dot `#ef4444`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.2)` border, `0.5rem` corner radius, and `14px` placeholder in `#3e403f` at 50% opacity. Focused inputs highlight with a `2px solid #248463` outer glow ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px footprint with `0.25rem` radius for checkboxes and circular geometry for radios. Active state fills with `#16654c` and displays a pure white check or inner dot.

### Cards & Ledger Modules
- **Metric Cards**: Structured container wrapped in Level 1 elevation, featuring top border accents colored in `#16654c` or `#248463` for active states. Quantitative metrics utilize tabular typography (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **Financial Voucher Snippet**: Dedicated voucher component featuring clean perforated horizontal dividers, micro-status tags, QR code verification modules, and monetary totals highlighted with `#f0fdf4` backdrop fills.

### Navigation & Header
- **Top Bar**: Sticky fixed header utilizing Level 3 glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), containing the dual-language SVG logo, inline route links with active emerald underlines, language toggle (AR/EN), and high-contrast "Request Demo / Download" CTA.
